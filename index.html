Royal International High School



Our school have been around for decades and we believe in educating minds, to pursue their dreams.



Education is compulsory. In the real world we live in, our students have to adapt to learning and see it as a
life long beneficial path, and a way of achieving the extra-ordinary and recognition they deserve.



Our Leaderships

We are dedicated in our journey to success, to become a leading institution in Sierra Leone and the world.

The Founders and the Tutors of Royal High International School.



Royal International High School Lab

Our dedicated students doing hands-on practicals, who knows what might go right or wrong, only these scientists that are brave enough to perform experiments will discover.

We learn by doing, not by spectating, and therefore we teach our students the craft of doing and force their brain to reasoning, in order to make great decisions that benefits them now and beyond.



Our Moments of Thanksgivings and Celebrations

Confidence plays a critical part, in the area of learning. It's the free will in believing in yourself.

Learning without fun can be boring, our institution offers these fun and engaging ceremonies for student to have fun and make good friends under elderly supervision.
